# American YouTube Skill

A Claude skill for planning and producing original YouTube content for a US English-speaking audience.

## Capabilities

- Channel positioning, niches, audience definition, and content pillars
- Video ideas, long-form scripts, and Shorts
- US English titles, thumbnail concepts, descriptions, and SEO
- Retention, CTR, analytics interpretation, and experiments
- Publishing cadence and monetization planning
- Copyright, sponsorship disclosure, and content-integrity reminders

## Install

Copy this repository's `SKILL.md` into a skill directory named `american-youtube` in your Claude skills folder, or import/package it using a skill installer that supports Claude skills.

## Example prompts

- “Create a 30-day content plan for my US personal-finance YouTube channel.”
- “Write a 7-minute video script in natural American English about beginner home workouts.”
- “Give me five accurate title options and three thumbnail concepts for this video.”

The skill does not guarantee views, virality, or revenue. Verify factual claims and content rights before publishing.
